The table and the split

These are the same sales as in Session 7: 6,212 from 2021 to 2023 to learn from, and the 1,409 of 2024 to check on.

Two scores

The first is the error in kroner. The second is the share of 2024 sales where a loan of 80 percent of the valuation is more than the price.

Session 7’s valuation

Ridge in logs, with alpha chosen on the folds: 1,257,437 kroner, and 15.1 percent of the loans underwater. Every new model is held to these two numbers.

A tree

Leaves of at least 20 sales were chosen on the folds. The tree is 13,000 kroner worse than ridge and puts 13.8 percent of the loans underwater.

A forest

A hundred trees, each grown on a bootstrap sample and averaged, cut the error by 144,000 kroner to 1,113,028. The underwater share falls to 12.1 percent.

Boosted trees, stopped on 2023

Depth 3 and 974 trees did best on the 2023 sales. Refitted on all three years, the model misses by 1,166,375 kroner, with 11.6 percent underwater.

Prices inside one town

Coordinates as columns

3 · Choosing a model

Part 3

When a more flexible model does better, what it costs to fit and to run, and what it can explain.

Four tables, four answers

table rows to learn from the linear model the best trees the change
volatility, test RMSE 1,894 days ridge 0.218 boosted trees 0.217 none
credit, test AUC 21,000 borrowers logistic regression 0.748 a forest 0.777 +0.029
houses, test RMSE 6,212 sales ridge 1.26 million boosted, coordinates 1.03 million -18 percent
hours, test AUC 17,422 hours logistic regression 0.833 a forest 0.857 +0.024

The trees gained most where location or a changing slope matters, and nothing where the target is mostly noise around a straight line.

Where the trees gained

A map The town columns hold one premium per town. Boosted trees cut the coordinates into districts and took 18 percent off the error.

A slope that changes A fall in the wind raises tomorrow’s price less often when today’s price is high. A linear model has one slope for it.

Steps On the credit table the default rate jumps from 12 to 30 percent at the first month late. A tree’s cut takes a step like that directly.

Mostly noise Next month’s volatility is close to this month’s plus noise. On 1,894 days the trees only matched ridge.

The time to fit and to predict

k-NN fits at once but takes half a second to score 2024; the forest takes over a second to fit. A grid of settings multiplies every fit.

Two fits, timed predict

Before you run it: which of the two takes longer in your browser, and by how much? Both models score about 0.857 on 2024.

Thirty more square metres

In logs, linear regression adds 14.7 percent to every house. Boosted trees add between 8 and 35 percent, depending on the house and where it stands.

A bigger house play

Boosted trees value the house lower at some larger areas, and the forest stops rising beyond the largest sales it learned from.

Monotone constraints

monotone_constraints lets the value only rise with the area. Every one of 200 test houses had a fall before and none has one now, for 33,000 kroner of RMSE.

Explanations in credit

The EU’s AI Act counts models that score the creditworthiness of people as high-risk. A person refused on such a score may ask for the main elements of the decision.

A coefficient states the same reason for every customer. A sum of a thousand trees has to be explained one decision at a time.

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act), Annex III, point 5(b), and Article 86(1).

None of them wins every column. Which one matters depends on who uses the model and how often it is refitted.

Choosing a model

  1. Score a baseline and a linear model first.
  2. Fit a forest and boosted trees on the same folds.
  3. Keep the flexible model only when it wins on the folds and on the test rows.
  4. Check what it does to one row: its direction, and far from the data.
  5. Price the compute: fitting, tuning and predicting.
  6. Ask who has to understand one prediction.

The routine behind every model

A baseline first The training average, the majority class or persistence. Every score goes next to the baseline’s.

A split that respects time When the rows are days or hours, the test rows come after the training rows.

Settings on validation rows Folds or a later block choose alpha, C, k, the depth and the number of trees.

The test rows once They score the model already chosen, and the folds are reported beside them.

A model read Its coefficients, its importance, or what it does to one row.

A model priced The time to fit and to predict, and who has to understand it.
